Vanessa Owusu Ansah

PhD Candidate in Economics · Lancaster University

I study how shocks, policies and economic opportunities shape the lives of vulnerable populations across generations.

My research lies at the intersection of development economics, gender and human capital, using microeconometrics and causal inference to produce evidence with people at its centre.
