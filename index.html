About me

Hi, You are welcome to my website!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Lancaster University, where my research sits at the intersection of development economics, gender, and human capital.

My work examines how shocks and interventions shape the long-run outcomes of vulnerable populations, with a primary focus on Sub-Saharan Africa and, more recently, developed country contexts. I use the tools of microeconometrics and causal inference to generate rigorous evidence on questions I care deeply about: how historical crises echo across generations, how economic opportunities shape the lives of vulnerable populations, and how institutions designed to protect them actually affect their outcomes.

Behind every analysis I run is a genuine conviction that the numbers in our data represent real lives, and that evidence done well is one of the most powerful tools we have for shaping policies that matter.
